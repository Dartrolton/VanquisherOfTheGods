# Vanquisher of the Gods

**A gameplay overhaul for God of War Ragnarök, with God of War (2018) brought back.**
Version 2.0 (early access), installed and configured with its own launcher.

**[Download the latest launcher](https://github.com/Dartrolton/VanquisherOfTheGods/releases/latest)** · [Nexus Mods page](https://www.nexusmods.com/godofwarragnarok/mods/260)

---

Hundreds of options, every one switchable from the launcher: combat, Rage, perks, companions, challenge modes, and God of War (2018) moves restored inside Ragnarök's own combat data.

## Highlights

- **Legacy Options:** God of War (2018) restored move by move. Bare-Handed combat with the guard stance, Agile Strike, the 2018 Blades of Chaos stance and Chaotic Rampage, Executioner's Cleave and the Spartan Stomp chain.
- **Shock Arrows:** 2018's third arrow returns for Atreus and Freya, with its shock, chain lightning, icon and electric bow.
- **Chaos Mode:** more enemies in every fight, brought in by the game's own encounters. They arrive with their entrances, are counted by the fight, and bosses are never copied.
- **Skill trees your way:** unlock every skill, finish Labors, and choose Empowered upgrades in the story.
- **Equipment page:** shields, rage modes, runics, relics, pommels, armor sets and resources, given to the game while it runs.
- **Combat and Rage:** weapon buffs, 2018 pommel effects, Scenario Buffs, and modified Rage modes.
- **Perks:** choose your starting perks, sorted by weapon and playstyle.
- **Challenge:** Burdens, Curse of Valhalla and Stronger Enemies.

## Requirements

- God of War Ragnarök for PC, game version 1.0.650.7780. Newer versions install with a warning.
- Windows 10 or 11.
- Optional: your own God of War (2018) installation, for options marked "uses your 2018 files". No 2018 files are included.

## Installation

1. Download **VanquisherOfTheGodsLauncher.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Dartrolton/VanquisherOfTheGods/releases/latest) and run it.
2. Select your game folder (the one with GoWR.exe).
3. Press **Install**, choose your settings, then **Play**.

The launcher includes its own script loader, updates itself, and backs up every file it replaces. **Uninstall** restores those backups, and your save files are never touched.

## Reporting a problem

Send **GOWR-Script-Loader.log** and **GOWR-Script-Loader-Crash.log** from your game folder, or use **Settings > Copy diagnostic info** in the launcher, together with what you were doing (story or Valhalla, which fight, which settings you changed).
